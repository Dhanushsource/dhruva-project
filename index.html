01 · EXPLORE STUDY PATHWAYS

Career domains.

Compare courses, typical entry routes and college examples. These institutions are examples, not rankings or endorsements.

⌘

Engineering & computing

Study routesB.E./B.Tech in computer science, electronics, civil, mechanical or other branches (typically 4 years); diploma routes may begin after Class 10 (often 3 years).

College examplesUVCE, Bengaluru, NITK Surathkal, and IIT Dharwad.

Typical entryClass 12 subject requirements and KCET, JEE or institution-specific admission routes vary.

✚

Medical · MBBS

Study routeMBBS is a medical degree after Class 12; it typically takes 5.5 years in India, including the compulsory internship.

College examplesBangalore Medical College and Research Institute and JSS Medical College, Mysuru.

Typical entryNEET-UG and counselling requirements apply. Check the official NTA portal.

✚

B.Sc. Nursing

Study routeA four-year undergraduate nursing programme prepares students for patient care, community health and clinical practice.

College exampleManipal College of Nursing, Manipal. Check current recognition and course status in the Indian Nursing Council directory.

Karnataka entranceKEA UGCET/CET notices describe the B.Sc. Nursing route; confirm the latest eligibility and selection rules each year.

Government vs private feesGovernment/KEA seats and private or management seats have different fee categories. Use the current KEA B.Sc. Nursing 2026 fee notice and confirm the seat type with the college.

◌

Agriculture & allied sciences

Study routesB.Sc. Agriculture and horticulture degrees are typically 4 years; agricultural engineering and related options have institution-specific structures.

College examplesUniversity of Agricultural Sciences, Dharwad and Keladi Shivappa Nayaka University of Agricultural and Horticultural Sciences, Shivamogga.

Karnataka entranceKEA CET (PCMB) is used for farm-university admissions; farmer-quota practical tests and ICAR quota have separate current rules.

Government vs private feesGovernment farm-university seats follow the current KEA/university schedule. Private or NRI seats, where offered, use separate institution and quota fees. Check the KEA UGCET 2026–27 fee structure and university notice.

◌

Forestry & wildlife studies

Study routesB.Sc. Forestry is typically 4 years; related paths include forest ecology, conservation, wildlife science and natural-resource management.

College exampleCollege of Forestry, Keladi Shivappa Nayaka University of Agricultural and Horticultural Sciences.

Karnataka entranceFarm-university UG admission uses KEA CET (PCMB); ICAR quota and course-specific requirements must be checked in that year’s notice.

Government vs private feesGovernment university fees are published through KEA/university notices. Forestry offerings and private-seat fees are institution-specific; verify recognition, seat quota and the KEA UGCET 2026–27 fee structure.

◉

Commerce & business

Study routesB.Com. and BBA programmes are commonly 3–4 years; options include economics, accounting, finance, entrepreneurship and business analytics.

College exampleIIM Bangalore BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship; many universities also offer B.Com. and BBA programmes.

Typical entryMany programmes accept students from different streams; check each institution's criteria.

§

Law & public policy

Study routesIntegrated B.A. LL.B. programmes are typically 5 years after Class 12; a 3-year LL.B. is another route after an undergraduate degree.

College exampleNational Law School of India University, Bengaluru.

Typical entryEntrance tests and eligibility differ across law schools; confirm the current admission notice.

✦

Arts & humanities

Study routesB.A. programmes are commonly 3–4 years, with subjects such as English, history, economics, psychology, languages, journalism and political science.

College exampleDr. Manmohan Singh Bengaluru City University lists undergraduate Arts and Visual Arts programmes; available majors differ by campus.

Career directionsResearch, education, media, communications, public service, policy and postgraduate study.

✦

Design & creative practice

Study routesB.Des. programmes are often 4 years; B.F.A. and related routes vary. Areas include communication, product, interaction, textile and visual design.

College exampleSrishti Manipal Institute of Art, Design and Technology.

Typical entrySome programmes ask for an aptitude test, interview or portfolio; review current course requirements.

College examples are for exploration, not rankings or endorsements. Verify course availability, recognition, eligibility, fees, entrance exams and admission dates on official institution and regulator pages.

COLLEGES · ADMISSIONS · COSTS

Find colleges & compare costs.

Use official directories and counselling notices to check current colleges, seat availability, ranks and fees.

▤

Karnataka PU colleges

Search recognized Pre-University colleges and confirm available combinations, medium, admission dates and fees with the institution.

Karnataka PU college directory ↗ Department of Pre-University Education ↗
⌘

Karnataka engineering colleges

KEA publishes UGCET admission, option-entry and allotment information. Compare the same course, category, quota, round and year when reading closing ranks.

Karnataka Examinations Authority ↗ JoSAA counselling for participating national institutes ↗
⚙

Diploma & polytechnic colleges

Check Karnataka DTE institution, admission and diploma allotment notices. DCET is used for eligible diploma-to-degree lateral-entry admissions.

Department of Technical Education, Karnataka ↗ KEA DCET admissions ↗
⚒

ITI colleges & trades

Search ITIs and approved trades in the NCVT system. Confirm the current trade, affiliation, intake, duration and any state admission process.

NCVT MIS institution and trade information ↗
▤

Karnataka degree colleges

Use the state student and university systems to find colleges, then verify affiliation, course, intake, recognition and fees on the university or college website.

Karnataka UUCMS ↗ All India Survey on Higher Education ↗
✚

India medical colleges · NEET

Check recognized institutions and course permissions, then use MCC or the relevant state counselling authority for seat matrices, allotments and closing ranks.

National Medical Commission ↗ Medical Counselling Committee ↗ Official NEET-UG portal ↗
✚

B.Sc. Nursing · Karnataka fees

KEA lists a course-specific 2026–27 fee notice. Compare government/KEA, private, management and NRI seat categories only within the same schedule; hostel and other charges may be separate.

KEA UGCET 2026 notices ↗ B.Sc. Nursing 2026–27 fee notice (PDF) ↗ Verify recognized nursing programmes ↗
◌

Agriculture & Forestry · Karnataka fees

Farm-university government seats, farmer quota, ICAR seats and any private/NRI offerings can have different admission and fee rules. Check the course-specific row and quota in current notices.

KEA UGCET cutoffs, seat matrices and notices ↗ UGCET 2026–27 provisional fee structure (PDF) ↗ UAS Dharwad UG admissions and ICAR notices ↗ KSNUAHS programmes and eligibility ↗
₹

How to compare ranks & fees

KCET and NEET closing ranks are not universal cutoffs. Compare by exam year, course, college, category, quota, counselling round and seat type.

Government, private, management, NRI and deemed-university fees can differ. Read the current fee order and counselling notice, and distinguish tuition from hostel and other charges.

✓

Check before applying

Confirm recognition, course approval, affiliation, total annual cost, refund rules, hostel fees, scholarship eligibility and official application dates directly with the authority.

Do not rely on an old cutoff as a promise of admission; ranks and fees change each year.

This project does not yet contain a complete college-by-college fee or cutoff database. The linked authorities publish the current source records; always use the latest official counselling round and fee notice.

FUND YOUR NEXT STEP

Scholarships & study abroad.

Find official starting points for central and state scholarships, plus international study and funding opportunities.

₹

Central Government scholarships

Search central-sector and centrally sponsored student schemes, review current notices, and apply through the official National Scholarship Portal when a scheme directs you there.

National Scholarship Portal ↗
⌂

State Government scholarships

State benefits can depend on domicile, course, income, community or category, and institution. Use the government scheme finder to select your state; Karnataka students can also check SSP.

myScheme · Central and State/UT schemes ↗ Karnataka State Scholarship Portal ↗
✈

International study & funding

Compare destinations, recognised programmes, costs, funding, language requirements, visas, and application timelines before choosing a course abroad.

EducationUSA · Study in the United States ↗ DAAD · Scholarships for Germany ↗ Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters ↗
✓

Prepare a strong application

Check eligibility and deadlines on the official scheme or university page. Keep marksheets, identity and domicile documents, income or category certificates when applicable, transcripts, passport, and references ready.

Never pay an unofficial agent or share passwords, OTPs, or banking PINs to “secure” a scholarship.

Scholarship availability, eligibility and closing dates change. Confirm the latest details with the official provider before applying.
